# Skinceptor

**Biological intelligence for beauty.**

**Live demo:** [skinceptor.tech](https://skinceptor.tech/)

---

## Overview

Beauty isn't just what you see. Every ingredient in your skincare routine sits on top of real chemistry, biology, and published science, but that layer is usually invisible to the people who care about it most.

Skinceptor changes that. It's an educational platform that maps **30+ beauty ingredients** to their underlying molecules, biological targets, genes, pathways, and cellular processes. These are visualized as interactive network graphs and backed by transparent, sourced evidence (PMIDs and DOIs) rather than a single opaque score.

## Features

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Ingredient explorer** | Chemical identity, interactive biology networks, and an evidence dashboard with study timelines and citations |
| **Biology maps** | Skin barrier, photoaging & collagen, melanogenesis, hair follicle cycling, epidermal turnover, and oxidative stress |
| **Research library** | Filterable literature by ingredient, pathway, and study design |
| **Compare** | Side-by-side biological comparison of two ingredients |
| **Claim checker** | Evaluates a beauty claim for biological plausibility, with an AI-powered mechanism analysis |
| **Ingredient checker** | Upload the ingredient list from a product to get information on each ingredient |
| **Biology as art** | Deterministic generative artwork derived from real scientific networks |

## Make it personal

With an account, Skinceptor becomes your own research workspace:

- **Saved ingredient library** with private notes
- **Saved searches and claims**
- **Reader-submitted evidence**
- **AI claim analysis**
- **Agent integrations**, so assistants like Claude or ChatGPT can search the dataset and read or write your library

## Evidence philosophy

- **Transparent sourcing:** every link in the biology is backed by cited evidence (PMIDs and DOIs).
- **No black-box scores:** you see the studies, not a single opaque rating.
- **Gaps are shown, not hidden:** where evidence is absent, Skinceptor displays a gap rather than inventing one.

## Important boundary

Skinceptor is **research navigation and educational biological intelligence**. It is **not** diagnosis, treatment, prescription, or a safety/efficacy rating. Consult a qualified professional for medical or dermatological advice.
